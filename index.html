The choice we are making

Premium enough to fund learning; accessible enough to create trial.

  • Positioning: clean energy and better taste for wellness professionals and fitness consumers—not a generic discount energy drink.
  • First channels: DTC for customer learning and gym & office for credible trial. Keep grocery selective until repeat is proven.
  • What we give up: €2.59’s unit margin and the immediate reach of a broad retail rollout.
  • Gate to expand: validate repeat purchase and improve blended LTV:CAC to at least 3:1 before committing to wider grocery distribution.
